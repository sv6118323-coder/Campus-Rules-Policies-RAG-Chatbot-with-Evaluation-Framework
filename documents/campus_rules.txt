ATTENDANCE POLICY:
- Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory for all students
- More than 3 consecutive absences requires a medical certificate
- Late entry not allowed after 10 minutes of class start
- Attendance below 75% will result in detention

DRESS CODE:
- Students must wear ID cards at all times on campus
- Formal dress is mandatory on Monday and Wednesday
- No sleeveless, torn, or inappropriate clothes allowed
- Sports dress only allowed on physical education days

EXAM RULES:
- No mobile phones allowed inside the exam hall
- Students must carry hall ticket and college ID card
- Malpractice leads to immediate cancellation of the exam
- Results will be withheld for students with dues

LIBRARY RULES:
- Maximum 2 books can be borrowed at a time
- Books must be returned within 14 days of borrowing
- Fine of Rs.2 per day charged for late returns
- Lost books must be replaced or paid for by the student

HOSTEL RULES:
- Curfew time is 9:00 PM for all hostel students
- No outside guests allowed in hostel rooms
- Ragging is strictly prohibited and is a punishable offence
- Mess timings: Breakfast 7-8AM, Lunch 12-1PM, Dinner 7-8PM

FEE POLICY:
- Tuition fees must be paid before the 10th of every month
- Late fee fine of Rs.50 per day after due date
- Fee receipts must be collected and kept safely
- No student will be allowed in exams if fees are pending
